# Geographic data sources

- `world.geo.json`: low-resolution world country boundaries from [`johan/world.geo.json`](https://github.com/johan/world.geo.json), derived from public-domain Natural Earth data.
- `US.geo.json`, `BR.geo.json`, `DE.geo.json`, `EG.geo.json`, `CN.geo.json`, and `AU.geo.json`: matching country extracts used for the six interactive research regions.

The boundaries are used only to organize and explore the film sample. They do not represent a finding of geographic emotional clustering or a position on disputed boundaries.
